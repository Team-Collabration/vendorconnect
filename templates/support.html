Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the instructions sent to your inbox. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder.
When will I receive my payment?
Payments are processed within 3-5 business days after order completion. You'll receive a notification when the payment is initiated and another when it's credited to your account.
How do I list a new product?
Go to Dashboard > Products > Add New Product. Fill in all required fields including title, description, price, images, and inventory. Click "Publish" when ready.
What are the commission rates?
Commission rates vary by category: Electronics (5-8%), Fashion (10-15%), Home & Kitchen (8-12%). You can view detailed rates in your seller dashboard under "Pricing & Fees".
How do I handle returns and refunds?
Returns must be processed within 7 days of request. Go to Orders > Returns, review the request, and either approve or reject with a valid reason. Refunds are automatically processed upon approval.
